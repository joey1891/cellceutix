BOTOX / FILLER

Botulinum Toxin

Muscle Relaxation / Wrinkle Reduction

Temporarily paralyzes muscles by inhibiting the secretion of acetylcholine, a neurotransmitter. Broadly applied for dynamic facial wrinkle improvement, masseter reduction, and body contouring.

HA Filler

Volumizing / Hydration

A substance that increases viscoelasticity in the body by cross-linking hyaluronic acid, a natural component of the human body. It excels in immediate volumization and is the gold standard filler, with proven high safety as it can be dissolved at any time using hyaluronidase.

SKIN BOOSTERS

PMMA

Polymethyl methacrylate

Features: Composed of non-degradable microspheres, providing semi-permanent effects. Induces the formation of a collagen capsule to fill volume deficits.

Watch Video 1 Watch Video 2

PLLA

Poly-L-lactic acid

Features: A biodegradable synthetic polymer that gradually degrades in the body, inducing robust autologous collagen production through a foreign body reaction. (e.g., Sculptra)

Watch Video

CaHA

Calcium Hydroxylapatite

Features: Composed of minerals found in bones and teeth. Provides immediate volume upon injection while simultaneously stimulating collagen production. (e.g., Radiesse)

Watch Video

PCL

Polycaprolactone

Features: A fully biodegradable material combining the immediate effects of HA fillers with the collagen-stimulating properties of PLLA. Offers an extended duration of 1 to 4 years. (e.g., Ellansé)

Watch Video

PDO

Polydioxanone

Features: An injectable form created by micro-pulverizing (microspheres) a highly safe material traditionally used for surgical dissolving sutures (lifting threads). (e.g., UltraCol)

Watch Video 1 Watch Video 2

PDLLA

Poly-D,L-lactic acid

Features: An isomer of PLLA featuring smooth, round particles with a porous, sponge-like internal structure, minimizing tissue irritation. (e.g., Juvelook)

ECM (Extracellular Matrix)

ECM Modulators

Extracellular Matrix (ECM) provides the essential structural and biochemical support to surrounding cells. By injecting or stimulating ECM components directly, these advanced therapies promote tissue regeneration, improve cellular environments, and fundamentally restore skin elasticity and overall health.
